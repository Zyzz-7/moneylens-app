MoneyLens Analytics

A private tool for the MoneyLens YouTube channel.

MoneyLens Analytics is a single-user tool. The owner of the MoneyLens YouTube channel runs it on their own computer to read that channel's own YouTube Analytics data and study how its videos perform.

It is not offered to the public. When the channel owner signs in with Google, it asks for read-only access only: viewing the YouTube account and viewing YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own content. It cannot upload, change, delete or post anything.
