Privacy

MoneyLens Analytics is a private tool. With read-only access, it reads the YouTube channel data and YouTube Analytics reports of the account that signs in, to analyse that channel's performance for its owner.

The data stays on the owner's computer. It may be analysed with the AI assistants the owner uses, and is never sold or shared with anyone else. Use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
