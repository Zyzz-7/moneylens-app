Privacy policy

MoneyLens Analytics · last updated 20 October 2026

What the app can access

When the owner of the MoneyLens YouTube channel signs in with Google, the app asks for two read-only permissions: viewing the YouTube account (the channel and its video list) and viewing YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own content. It cannot upload, change, delete or post anything.

How the data is used

Only to analyse how the MoneyLens channel's videos perform, for the channel's owner. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

Where the data is kept

The data and the Google access token are stored on the owner's own computer. There is no server for this app. The owner may analyse their own data with the AI assistants they use. It is not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MoneyLens Analytics' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.

This website

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Through the MoneyLens YouTube channel.
